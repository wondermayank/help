Yes — always. Skipping moisturiser when you have oily skin triggers your skin to produce even more oil to compensate. The key is choosing the right texture: opt for a lightweight, oil-free gel moisturiser with ingredients like niacinamide or hyaluronic acid rather than heavy creams. Proper hydration actually helps regulate sebum production over time.
Yes — and they actually work well together. An older concern about niacinamide converting retinol into niacin (causing flushing) has been thoroughly debunked at concentrations found in skincare products. Niacinamide can actually help soothe the irritation that sometimes comes with starting retinol. Apply retinol first, wait for it to absorb, then apply niacinamide.
Yes — even indoors. UVA rays that cause premature ageing and hyperpigmentation penetrate glass windows. Consistent daily SPF is the single most impactful thing you can do for your skin long-term, more effective than any serum. Use at least SPF 30, broad-spectrum, as the final step of your morning routine. This is non-negotiable regardless of skin type.
The golden rule: thinnest to thickest texture. Morning: cleanser → toner/essence → serum → eye cream → moisturiser → SPF. Night: oil cleanser → water cleanser → exfoliant (2-3x weekly) → treatment serum → moisturiser. Wait 30-60 seconds between steps for better absorption. Never apply SPF before moisturiser — it goes last in the morning, always.
Patience is the most important skincare ingredient. Hydration improvements are visible in days. Barrier repair takes 2-4 weeks. Acne reduction with salicylic acid or niacinamide takes 4-8 weeks. Hyperpigmentation fading and retinol anti-aging benefits take 12+ weeks. Introduce one new product at a time and keep a routine consistent for at least 28 days (one full skin cell turnover cycle) before judging results.
No — more steps do not mean better results. The K-beauty philosophy is about skin health and layering light hydration rather than using every step daily. A well-chosen 3-5 step routine beats an inconsistent 10-step one every time. The most important steps are: cleanser, moisturiser, and SPF (morning). Everything else is optional and depends on your specific concerns. Consistency beats complexity.
