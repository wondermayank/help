FAQ

About MehndiVault

What is MehndiVault?

MehndiVault is a hand-curated gallery of mehndi and henna designs, featuring thousands of patterns across Bridal, Hand, Feet, and Hijab styles. Created by Wondermayank, it is a free resource for mehndi inspiration.

What types of mehndi designs are available?

The collection includes Bridal mehndi designs, Hand patterns (Arabic, Indian, Indo-Arabic), Feet mehndi designs, Hijab mehndi, and Hijab Fashion patterns — spanning traditional and contemporary styles.

Can I download or save mehndi designs?

Yes. You can save designs to your personal favourites collection by tapping the heart icon on any design card. From the lightbox view, you can also download the design image directly to your device.

Where does the design collection come from?

The image data is sourced from the open-source BehindMask/Mehndi-Design-Book repository on GitHub, curated and presented by Wondermayank through this gallery interface.

Does MehndiVault work offline?

MehndiVault supports installation as a Progressive Web App (PWA). Once installed on your device, the app shell and previously loaded designs are accessible even when offline.
